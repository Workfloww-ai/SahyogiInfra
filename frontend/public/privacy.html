Sahyogi Infra Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

SahYogi InfraCare Pvt. Ltd.

SahYogi InfraCare Pvt. Ltd. (“SahYogi,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you provide to us through our website, sahyogi.net.in.

1.Information Collection

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. Our website does not automatically track your digital data, use cookies for profiling, or collect background browsing history. Information is collected through:

  • Contact/Enquiry Forms: Name, organization, email, and specific service interests.
  • Direct Communication: Information sent via email to our business addresses for recruitment or hospitality enquiries.

2.Purpose of Data Use

The information you share is used strictly for professional business purposes:

  • To respond to your service enquiries or requests for quotes.
  • To evaluate candidates for staffing mandates.
  • To coordinate property management or hospitality operations.

3.Data Protection & Sharing

We treat your professional information as confidential.

  • No Third-Party Sells: Your contact information is never sold or traded to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Professional Necessity: Information is only shared with internal teams or prospective clients (in the case of staffing) as required to fulfil your specific request.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by Indian law or to protect our legal rights.

4.Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to social media profiles or external partners. Please note that we do not control the privacy practices of these external sites.

5.Data Security

While our website is a static platform, we maintain internal security protocols to ensure that the data you send via email or forms is stored securely once it reaches our local servers and is protected from unauthorized access.
